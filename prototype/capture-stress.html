What the 32 tests agree on

  • One friction dominates (~26 of 32): the field is not focused on open and sits at the bottom — every capture pays a look-down + tap before the first keystroke. This is the whole gap vs. Apple Notes.
  • Precision fails hands-first (~6): gloves, wet, cold, or sweaty hands miss the small target. A bigger hit area fixes it without any new feature.
  • Text can't win 2 cases (driving, shower) — eyes and hands are fully committed. Honest verdict: don't pretend to; those need voice, which is out of scope.
  • Burst capture already works — focus persists after Enter — but autofocus is what makes the first line as fast as the rest.
Verdict: As-is, Capture ties Apple Notes — both cost an open + a tap. It passes ("faster than Notes") only with one pure-speed change: autofocus the field on open + keep it focused after Enter, plus a larger tap target for hands-first cases. That single change is applied in Capture →. No search, AI, weekly review, or Executive until this holds.
